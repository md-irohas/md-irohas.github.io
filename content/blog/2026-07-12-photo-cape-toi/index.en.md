## Story

In March 2026, after stopping by [Aoshima Shrine](/blog/2026-05-30-photo-aoshima-jinja/) and [Udo Jingu](/blog/2026-07-03-photo-udo-jingu/) in Miyazaki Prefecture, I headed to Cape Toi.

Cape Toi is located in Kushima, Miyazaki Prefecture.
Near the tip of the cape are sites such as Cape Toi Lighthouse and Misaki Shrine.
The cape's grasslands are also home to [Misaki horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misaki_horse), a native Japanese horse breed designated as a National Natural Monument of Japan.

### Cape Toi

Cape Toi is some distance from the nearest urban area.

I drove south from the direction of Nichinan to reach the cape.

After passing through the entrance gate and driving for a while, I came to Komatsugaoka Square on the right.
I parked there and walked toward the sea, where an expansive view opened up before me.

### Cape Toi Lighthouse

My next stop was Cape Toi Lighthouse.

A horse blocking the road caused a brief delay along the way, but I eventually made it safely to the lighthouse parking lot.

The lighthouse stands on high ground, and the parking lot itself offers a wide-open view.

### Misaki Shrine

After walking around Cape Toi Lighthouse, I headed to nearby Misaki Shrine.

From a fork in the road on the way to the lighthouse, I drove downhill for a few minutes.
I parked the car and continued through the torii gate.
