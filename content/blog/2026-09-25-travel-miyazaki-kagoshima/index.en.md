## Story

In March 2026, I took about two weeks off and set out on a road trip from Tokyo to my family home in Nagasaki, just as I had [the previous year](/blog/2025-08-29-trip-mie-wakayama-nagasaki-yamaguchi/).

This time, I explored Miyazaki and Kagoshima along the way.
In Miyazaki, I visited coastal capes and shrines. In Kagoshima, I spent a day on each of the Satsuma and Osumi peninsulas.

This post covers the outward journey from Tokyo to Nagasaki.
I plan to write about the return trip in a separate post.


## Timeline

The outward journey took seven days and covered approximately 2,650 km.

I spent the first two days heading west on the expressways and reached Kyushu on Day 3.
After exploring Miyazaki and Kagoshima from Days 3 to 6, I drove to my family home in Nagasaki on Day 7.

After exploring both places, I headed farther south.

<br>
<br>

I had originally planned to stay in Miyazaki City, but there were no hotel rooms available, so I continued to nearby Miyakonojo, a city in southern Miyazaki Prefecture, instead.

<br>
<br>

Miyakonojo is just under an hour from Miyazaki City by expressway.
Hotel rooms seemed easier to find there, so it might be a useful alternative when traveling by car.

<br>
<br>

After relaxing at a hot spring facility, I had a chicken nanban set meal at its restaurant and stayed overnight at a hotel in Miyakonojo.

<br>

After visiting Chiran Peace Museum, I walked around Cape Nagasakibana, relaxed at Tamatebako Onsen, and continued to Kagoshima City.

<br>
<br>

<br>

Once in the city, I stopped at <a href="https://store.starbucks.co.jp/detail-1441/" target="_blank">Starbucks Kagoshima Sengan-en</a>.
Sengan-en is the former villa of the Shimazu clan, the lords of the Satsuma domain, and the café is housed in a historic building with a lovely atmosphere. I would recommend a visit.

<br>
<br>

I had Kagoshima ramen for dinner.
